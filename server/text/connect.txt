{{B}}Set up a new device{{N}}
1. In Helium open helium://extensions, turn on "Developer mode", click "Load unpacked"
   and pick this folder (copy it to the device first if it's another machine):
@lxc      the extension/ folder of the project
@docker      {{PROJECT_DIR}}/extension
2. Pin the extension (puzzle icon), click it, and enter:
   Server URL: one of
{{ADDRESSES}}
   Token:      {{TOKEN}}
   Device name: anything, e.g. "Laptop"
3. Click "Test connection", then "Save". Existing history: click "Upload existing history".
{{D}}http:// is fine on this machine, your LAN or Tailscale. Over the internet, put the server behind
HTTPS (see README, "Caddy").{{N}}
